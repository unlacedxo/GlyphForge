Procreate Workflow

1. Screenshot

Screenshot the blueprint and crop it square.

2. Import

Add it to Procreate, lower opacity to 20–35%, and lock the guide layer.

3. Trace Big Shapes

Outer frame → core symbol → eyes/details → lockup → glow/shadow.

4. Render Last

Texture, shine, and glow come after the silhouette reads clearly.
